    Appendix A. Formula Sheet

    The following equations and formulas are included on the formula sheet provided during exams for this course.
    🔗

    Basic Derivatives.

    \begin{align*} \frac{d}{dx}(x^n) &= nx^{n-1}, & \frac{d}{dx}(e^x) &= e^x,\\ \frac{d}{dx}(a^x) &= a^x\ln a, & \frac{d}{dx}(\ln x) &= \frac{1}{x},\\ \frac{d}{dx}(\sin x) &= \cos x, & \frac{d}{dx}(\cos x) &= -\sin x,\\ \frac{d}{dx}(\tan x) &= \sec^2 x, & \frac{d}{dx}(\sec x) &= \sec x\tan x. \end{align*}
    🔗

    Trigonometric Values in Radians.

    🔗

    Trigonometric Identities.

    \begin{align*} \sin^2 x+\cos^2 x &= 1,\\ 1+\tan^2 x &= \sec^2 x,\\ 1+\cot^2 x &= \csc^2 x. \end{align*}
    \begin{align*} \tan x &= \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}, & \sec x &= \frac{1}{\cos x},\\ \csc x &= \frac{1}{\sin x}, & \cot x &= \frac{1}{\tan x}. \end{align*}
    🔗

    Double-Angle Formulas and Variants.

    \begin{align*} \sin(2x) &= 2\sin x\cos x,\\ \cos(2x) &= \cos^2x-\sin^2x,\\ \cos^2x &= \frac{1}{2}(1+\cos(2x)),\\ \sin^2x &= \frac{1}{2}(1-\cos(2x)). \end{align*}
    🔗

    Exponent Rules.

    \begin{align*} a^m a^n &= a^{m+n},\\ \frac{a^m}{a^n} &= a^{m-n},\\ (a^m)^n &= a^{mn}. \end{align*}
    🔗

    Logarithm Rules.

    \begin{align*} \ln(ab) &= \ln a+\ln b,\\ \ln\left(\frac{a}{b}\right) &= \ln a-\ln b,\\ \ln(a^n) &= n\ln a. \end{align*}
